# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Rulefall a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology preference.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and claims rather than people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes without blame.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the boundaries of maintainers and contributors.
- Accept moderation decisions and disengage when asked.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Bad-faith manipulation of benchmarks, evidence, community votes, stars, or discussions.
- Spam, coordinated promotion, or other conduct that violates a platform's rules.
- Any other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/LyraZeta/rulefall/security/advisories/new); begin the report title with `[Conduct]` so it can be routed correctly. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. If that channel is unavailable, wait for it to be restored rather than disclosing private information publicly.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as circumstances allow. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when a person is officially representing Rulefall in public spaces.
